# ORBIT 1.0.0

The first public release of ORBIT, a visual, local-first productivity workspace for people juggling several projects at once.

- **Universe:** your work as a zoomable night sky. Groups are galaxies laid out on a spiral, projects are star systems, tasks are planets sized by effort and coloured by priority. Click to fly in, Esc to fly out, drag stars between galaxies.
- **Context parking:** park a task with where you stopped and your next action, and pick it up later exactly there.
- **Focus timer:** presets, flow mode and breaks. It counts into overtime instead of forcing you to stop, and reloads or sleep never knock it out.
- **Today:** a morning / afternoon / evening day board with drag and drop.
- **Capture and Inbox:** `C` from anywhere; sort later.
- **What next? and Insights:** transparent, rule-based suggestions and charts. No streaks, no scores, no AI service.

No account, no tracking. Your data stays on your computer; Settings → Back up saves it as one file that any edition can restore.

## Downloads

- `ORBIT-Setup-1.0.0.exe`: Windows installer, per user, no admin rights needed. Recommended.
- `ORBIT-1.0.0-Portable.exe`: one Windows exe, nothing installed. Starts a few seconds slower because it unpacks itself each time. Shares its data with the installed app.
- `ORBIT-1.0.0.html`: the single-file edition for any modern browser (Chrome or Edge recommended). It keeps its data in that browser.
- `ORBIT-1.0.0-src.zip`: the source, MIT licensed.

The Windows builds are not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say it "protected your PC". Choose **More info → Run anyway**. Checksums are in `SHA256SUMS.txt`.
